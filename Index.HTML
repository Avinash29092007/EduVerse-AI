About the Project

Built by AI & Data Science students, for students

EduVerse AI is a final-year innovation project from the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at T.J.S Engineering College. This prototype demonstrates the frontend experience — the AI predictions you see are demo data, ready to be powered by a Flask + scikit-learn backend.
